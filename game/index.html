made by Shreyash · @ShreyashAtX
How it works

A game, learned by watching

  1. Bots play the real gameLike this one, live: a kid bot against the learned monster. About forty thousand games.
  2. Each moment becomes numbersEvery 50 ms the whole house is 311 numbers, plus the keys pressed. The strip below is this game's.
  3. A network learns what comes nextA transformer with 25 million weights sees one moment and the keys, and predicts the next.
  4. Then it is the gameIn GameNet nothing else runs. Your keys and the monster's brain go in; the house comes out, ten times a second.
