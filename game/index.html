Candles

The house is dark but for its candles. Blow every one out. Something lives here, it is faster than you, and across the table a giant is playing it.

A small horror game, and an experiment: I wrote the game, let bots play it about forty thousand times, and trained a neural network on those games until it could run the house by itself.

how it was made ↓

How it was made

  1. Write the gameRules in code: walls, doors, candles, the giant's searchlight and hand.
  2. Let bots playAbout 40,000 games of kid bots against the monster. Every 50 ms is saved as 311 numbers and the keys pressed.
  3. Train the worldA transformer of 25 million weights learns one thing: this moment and the keys give the next moment.
  4. Train the monsterA second, small network learns to hunt by reinforcement learning. It catches 97% of the bots.

In Classic the house is code. In GameNet it is the network, running on your graphics card, and nothing else decides what happens: if it says the door is open, it is open. The monster's brain is the same in both, so the two houses are hunted by the same thing.
